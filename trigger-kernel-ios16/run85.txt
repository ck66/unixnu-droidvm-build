run85 2026-11-26: mirror the cryptex /usr/lib so libobjc can load its trampolines.

run84's DYLD_ROOT_PATH changed nothing: dyld ignores root-path overrides for platform
binaries, so every daemon still aborts with

  couldn't dlopen libobjc-trampolines.dylib:
  dlopen(/usr/lib/libobjc-trampolines.dylib, 0x0106): tried: (no such file, not in dyld cache)

The dylib is inside the cryptex (mounted at /private/preboot).  On a real device launchd
bindfs-mounts the cryptex directories onto the root; that glue is absent here (the log shows
`BindFS bindfs_mount: lookup of /private/preboot/<hash>/usr/standalone/firmware failed`),
so /usr/lib never sees the cryptex tree.

This build, right after the cryptex mount is verified and still inside launchd's exec path
(where the bindfs entitlement is held), bindfs-mounts <cryptex>/usr/lib onto /usr/lib.
