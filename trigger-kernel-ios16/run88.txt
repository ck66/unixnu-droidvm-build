run88 2026-11-26: the mirror landed and broke the exec of init.

run87's entitlement change worked -- with it the mount returns 0 -- but the guest then panics in
load_init_program with OS_REASON_EXEC (SIGKILL of init): bindfs cannot union, so covering the
root's /usr/lib with the cryptex copy hides dyld and libSystem.

This build probes the cryptex first and only mirrors when <cryptex>/usr/lib carries dyld and
libSystem.B.dylib, recording

  native_owner_cache_bind_dyld        vnode_lookup(<cryptex>/usr/lib/dyld)
  native_owner_cache_bind_libsystem   vnode_lookup(<cryptex>/usr/lib/libSystem.B.dylib)
  native_owner_cache_bind_status      mount result (0 attempted/failed as reported, -1 = skipped)

so the next step is decided by what the cryptex actually contains rather than by another guess.
