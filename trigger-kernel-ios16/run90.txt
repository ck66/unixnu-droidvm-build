run90 2026-11-26: the redirect works, the sandbox refuses the path it points at.

run89 redirected libobjc's absolute dlopen at the cryptex copy under /private/preboot and the
error changed from "(no such file, not in dyld cache)" to "(errno=1, not in dyld cache)" --
errno 1 is EPERM: the file is now found, and the sandbox is what refuses it, because nothing
may read under /private/preboot.

So the cryptex root is bindfs-mirrored at /System/Cryptexes/OS instead -- the path Apple's own
profiles allow -- and the redirect points at that copy:

  /usr/lib/libobjc-trampolines.dylib ->
      /System/Cryptexes/OS/usr/lib/libobjc-trampolines.dylib

The instrumented globals now also record vnode_lookup("/System/Cryptexes/OS") so the next
reading says whether that mount point exists in this image.
