The legacy family forwards every selector to [this+0xd8]->[+0xd8]->vtable[slot], and
that chain ends on the GOP nub, an IOService: this image carries no
IOMobileFramebuffer/AppleDisplay vtable at all. So every answer the compositor gets is
a value read by an unrelated method. This build records what each selector actually
returned (first/second scalar output, the sel 3 port) and, under legacy-size=1, answers
sel 8 with the real panel geometry.
